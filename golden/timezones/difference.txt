# timezones.difference (docs/spec/state.md). Each exchange is its own
# conversation unless the turns follow one another with no blank line.
#
# The reply names the second place against the first ("Tokio va 8 horas por
# delante de Madrid"); in "how far ahead is X of Y" the place asked about is
# the second. A change of summer time at either place within 30 days that
# changes the difference is added, with its date (Europe changes on 28 March
# 2027, Australia on 4 April). A yes/no question gets yes or no.
#
# The remembered place is not stored yet (the `preferences` skill), so the
# exchanges that use it are run with the caller's place instead, the next
# reference place in state.md's precedence.
#
# Given: now defaults to 2027-03-14 18:42 Europe/Madrid (a Sunday).

[es] U: ¿cuántas horas de diferencia hay entre Madrid y Tokio?
     T: Tokio va 8 horas por delante de Madrid. Desde el 28 de marzo serán 7.
                                                  → answered · timezones.difference · from=Madrid to=Tokio

[es] U: ¿qué diferencia horaria hay entre España y Argentina?
     T: Buenos Aires va 4 horas por detrás de Madrid. Desde el 28 de marzo serán 5.
                                                  → answered · timezones.difference · from=España to=Argentina
