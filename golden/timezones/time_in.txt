# timezones.time_in (docs/spec/state.md). Each exchange is its own
# conversation unless the turns follow one another with no blank line.
#
# Times are written `HH:MM` in both languages, as state.md writes them; a
# place a day ahead or behind the reference date (the turn's own clock here,
# nothing stored) gets "(ya es lunes)" / "(already Monday)". Several places
# with the same time share one sentence. A country stands for its capital
# and the reply names the city; Spain gives both its zones.
#
# Left out, with the reason:
#   The remembered-place exchanges ("Given: stored place = Sevilla"): no
#     preferences are stored yet (the `preferences` skill); "mi ciudad" with
#     nothing stored asks back, as below.
#   "¿y allí qué hora es?" after Tokio was named: the engine does not yet
#     keep the last place named in a conversation, so "allí" asks back.
#
# Given: now defaults to 2027-03-14 18:42 Europe/Madrid (a Sunday).

[es] U: ¿qué hora es en Tokio?
     T: En Tokio son las 02:42 (ya es lunes).     → answered · timezones.time_in · place=Tokio

[es] U: q hora es en nueva york
     T: En Nueva York son las 13:42.              → answered · timezones.time_in · place=Nueva York

[es] U: oye, dime la hora de Londres anda
     T: En Londres son las 17:42.                 → answered · timezones.time_in · place=Londres

[es] U: ¿Qué hora es ahora mismo en Buenos Aires?
     T: En Buenos Aires son las 14:42.            → answered · timezones.time_in · place=Buenos Aires

[es] U: hora en pekin
     T: En Pekín es la 01:42 (ya es lunes).       → answered · timezones.time_in · place=Pekín

[es] U: ¿me puedes decir qué hora es en Sidney?
     T: En Sídney son las 04:42 (ya es lunes).    → answered · timezones.time_in · place=Sídney
     (Sidney is accepted as a spelling of Sídney)

     T: ¿En qué ciudad?                           → asks · timezones.time_in · place

# The user's own place, with nothing stored (state.md, the longer exchange).

# Summer time follows the turn's clock: in July New York is UTC-4, Madrid
# UTC+2 and Sydney UTC+10 (its winter).
Given: now = 2027-07-01 12:00 Europe/Madrid
[es] U: qué hora es en Nueva York
     T: En Nueva York son las 06:00.              → answered · timezones.time_in · place=Nueva York

[en] U: what time is it in Sydney
     T: In Sydney it's 20:00.                     → answered · timezones.time_in · place=Sydney

# Just after Europe's change on 28 March: London is on summer time too.
Given: now = 2027-03-28 03:30 Europe/Madrid
[es] U: qué hora es en Londres
     T: En Londres son las 02:30.                 → answered · timezones.time_in · place=Londres

# The reference date is the turn's own: at 00:30 in Madrid, New York is
# still on the day before.
Given: now = 2027-03-15 00:30 Europe/Madrid
[en] U: what time is it in New York
     T: In New York it's 19:30 (still Sunday).    → answered · timezones.time_in · place=New York
