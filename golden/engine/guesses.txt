# A guess that would change a preference only asks; a yes stores it, a no
# stores nothing (DESIGN.md section 11).
[en] U: I usually go by Liz
     T: Do you want me to call you Liz?           → asks · preferences.set · confirm
[en] U: yes
     T: Nice to meet you, Liz. I'll remember that.
                                                  → answered · preferences.set · confirm=yes key=name value=Liz
[en] U: what's my name?
     T: Your name is Liz.                         → answered · preferences.get · key=name

[en] U: I usually go by Liz
     T: Do you want me to call you Liz?           → asks · preferences.set · confirm
[en] U: no
     T: Okay, nothing stored.                     → answered · preferences.set · confirm=no key=name value=Liz
[en] U: what's my name?
     T: I don't know your name.                   → answered · preferences.get · key=name
