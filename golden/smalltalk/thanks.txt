# "no, gracias" / "no thanks" are refusals, never "de nada". The spec's
# `unhandled` reply needs the dialog layer; here nothing recognises them.
[es] U: no, gracias
     T: No te he entendido.                       → unhandled · catch-all

[en] U: no thanks
     T: I didn't understand that.                 → unhandled · catch-all
