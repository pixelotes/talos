# Not a bare greeting: a greeting followed by something else is left to the
# rule that recognises the rest; with no such rule yet, nothing answers.
[es] U: hola, qué hora es
     T: No sé hacer eso.                          → unhandled · catch-all

[es] U: buenas noches
     T: No sé hacer eso.                          → unhandled · catch-all

[es] U: hola, ¿qué tal?
     T: No sé hacer eso.                          → unhandled · catch-all

[en] U: good night
     T: I can't do that.                          → unhandled · catch-all

[en] U: hello, what's 3 times 4
     T: I can't do that.                          → unhandled · catch-all

# A courtesy followed by a recognised request is dropped: here the request
# is itself a greeting.

     T: ¡Buenas tardes!                           → answered · smalltalk.greet
