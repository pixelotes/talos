# Not farewells: a plan with a destination goes on to the rest of the pipeline
# (the spec says `deflected`, which only the reflector produces; until it
# exists nothing answers), and so does "no, déjalo" (dialog layer).
[es] U: me voy a Sevilla el viernes
     T: No te he entendido.                       → unhandled · catch-all

[en] U: I'm off to Paris
     T: You're off to Paris?                      → deflected · plan
