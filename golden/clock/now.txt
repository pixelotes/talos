# clock.now (docs/spec/basics.md). Each exchange is its own conversation, so
# every reply is the canonical wording.
#
# Spanish writes no zero before hours 1 to 9 ("Es la 1:05", "Son las 9:05")
# and says the singular only for hour one; English pads ("02:42"). Seconds
# are dropped, never rounded up.
#
# Left out, with the reason:
#   "qué hora es en Tokio", "what time is it in New York": timezones.time_in
#     (another skill; no rule of clock reaches them).
#   "avísame en 5 minutos": the spec's outcome is a bare `unhandled` with no
#     intent, which no clock rule produces; the catch-all answers it.
#
# Given: now defaults to 2027-03-14 18:42 Europe/Madrid.

[es] U: qué hora es
     T: Son las 18:42.                            → answered · clock.now

[es] U: me dices la hora?
     T: Son las 18:42.                            → answered · clock.now

[es] U: k hora es
     T: Son las 18:42.                            → answered · clock.now

[es] U: hora
     T: Son las 18:42.                            → answered · clock.now

[es] U: tienes hora
     T: Son las 18:42.                            → answered · clock.now

[es] U: dime la hora porfa
     T: Son las 18:42.                            → answered · clock.now

[es] U: ¿Qué hora es, por favor?
     T: Son las 18:42.                            → answered · clock.now

[es] U: qué hora es aquí
     T: Son las 18:42.                            → answered · clock.now

# A greeting in front is dropped.
[es] U: hola, qué hora es
     T: Son las 18:42.                            → answered · clock.now

[es] U: qué hora será dentro de 3 horas
     T: Dentro de 3 horas serán las 21:42.        → answered · clock.now · offset=3 h

[es] U: qué hora será en 20 minutos
     T: Dentro de 20 minutos serán las 19:02.     → answered · clock.now · offset=20 min

[es] U: qué hora será dentro de una hora y media
     T: Dentro de 1 hora y 30 minutos serán las 20:12.
                                                  → answered · clock.now · offset=1 h 30 min

[es] U: qué hora será dentro de media hora
     T: Dentro de 30 minutos serán las 19:12.     → answered · clock.now · offset=30 min

[es] U: qué hora será dentro de 6 horas
     T: Dentro de 6 horas serán las 00:42 de mañana.
                                                  → answered · clock.now · offset=6 h

# More than a day ahead names the date.
[es] U: qué hora será dentro de 2 días
     T: Dentro de 2 días serán las 18:42 (martes, 16 de marzo de 2027).
                                                  → answered · clock.now · offset=2 d

[es] U: qué hora será dentro de unas cosas
     T: No he entendido ese plazo. Prueba con «dentro de 3 horas».
                                                  → unhandled · clock.now · offset=dentro de unas cosas

# An offset Talos cannot read gets a hint, and `unhandled`.

     T: I couldn't read that interval. Try “in 3 hours”.
                                                  → unhandled · clock.now · offset=in 3 fortnights
