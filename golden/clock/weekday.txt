# clock.weekday (docs/spec/basics.md). Each exchange is its own conversation.
#
# The date the weekday was asked about is the `date` slot, in ISO. A date
# before 1583 is answered in the proleptic Gregorian calendar and the reply
# says so. An ambiguous English `3/4` is asked back as a choice: "Do you
# mean 3 April or 4 March?".
#
# Given: now defaults to 2027-03-14 18:42 Europe/Madrid.

[es] U: qué día de la semana es hoy
     T: Hoy es domingo (14 de marzo).             → answered · clock.weekday · date=2027-03-14

[es] U: qué día cae el 25 de diciembre
     T: El 25 de diciembre de 2027 es sábado.     → answered · clock.weekday · date=2027-12-25

# Numeric dates in English are ambiguous: Talos asks which, as a choice.
# 25/12 and 12/25 have only one reading.

     T: Do you mean 3 April or 4 March?           → asks · clock.weekday · date

     T: 4 March 2027 was a Thursday.              → answered · clock.weekday · date=2027-03-04
