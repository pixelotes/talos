# clock.until (docs/spec/basics.md, and docs/spec/conversation.md 2.3 for
# times on a named day). Each exchange is its own conversation.
#
# Direction comes from the verb: "falta", "until" look forward; "hace",
# "desde", "since" look back. A date without a year is the next occurrence
# (today included) for "until" and the most recent one for "since". The
# target on the outcome line is the resolved date, or `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` for
# a time.
#
# "cuánto falta para mi cumpleaños", "how long until" and "how long until
# 3/4" ask back; the next message answers, and the original request is
# completed (docs/spec/conversation.md 3.2 and 3.4). The same second turns
# are also asked directly below.
#
# Left out, with the reason:
#   "how long is the flight to Lisbon": not a date computation; no rule of
#     clock matches, and the spec's outcome is a bare `unhandled`.
#
# Given: now defaults to 2027-03-14 18:42 Europe/Madrid.
