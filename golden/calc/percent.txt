# calc.percent (docs/spec/basics.md, `calc`).

# Examples

[es] U: el 15% de 80
     T: El 15 % de 80 es 12.                      → answered · calc.percent · amount=80 percent=15

[es] U: qué porcentaje es 20 de 50
     T: 20 es el 40 % de 50.                      → answered · calc.percent · part=20 whole=50

[es] U: 80 mas un 15%
     T: 80 más un 15 % es 92.                     → answered · calc.percent · amount=80 percent=+15

[es] U: 50 euros con un 20% de descuento
     T: Con un 20 % de descuento, 50 € se quedan en 40 €.
                                                  → answered · calc.percent · amount=50 percent=-20

     T: El 0 % de 80 es 0.                        → answered · calc.percent · amount=80 percent=0

     T: 150% of 80 is 120.                        → answered · calc.percent · amount=80 percent=150

Given: hint = none

     T: El 15 % de -80 es -12.                    → answered · calc.percent · amount=-80 percent=15

Given: hint = en

[en] U: what percent is 5 of 0
     T: A percentage of zero is undefined.        → answered · calc.percent · part=5 whole=0

Given: hint = none

[es] U: de 0 a 5 cuánto ha subido en porcentaje
     T: Subir desde 0 no se puede expresar en porcentaje.
                                                  → answered · calc.percent · from=0 to=5

Given: hint = en

[en] U: percent change from 80 to 60
     T: That's a 25% decrease.                    → answered · calc.percent · from=80 to=60

[en] U: percent change from 7 to 7
     T: There is no change.                       → answered · calc.percent · from=7 to=7

[en] U: decrease 100 by 100%
     T: 100 minus 100% is 0.                      → answered · calc.percent · amount=100 percent=-100

Given: hint = none

[es] U: el 15% de 99,99
     T: El 15 % de 99,99 es ≈ 15.                 → answered · calc.percent · amount=99.99 percent=15
     (14,9985 rounded to two decimals is 15,00, shown as ≈ 15)

[es] U: descuento del 30% sobre 59,90
     T: Con un 30 % de descuento, 59,9 se quedan en 41,93.
                                                  → answered · calc.percent · amount=59.9 percent=-30

[es] U: 100 más el 21% de IVA
     T: 100 más un 21 % de IVA es 121.            → answered · calc.percent · amount=100 percent=+21
     (the user gave the rate)

Given: hint = en

[en] U: 80 + 15%
     T: 80 plus 15% is 92.                        → answered · calc.percent · amount=80 percent=+15
     (a trailing percent after a plus is read as an increase)

[en] U: 100 plus 21% VAT
     T: 100 plus 21% VAT is 121.                  → answered · calc.percent · amount=100 percent=+21

[en] U: 20 is 0% of what
     T: No number does: 0% of anything is 0.      → answered · calc.percent · part=20 percent=0

[en] U: percent change from -50 to -25
     T: That's a 50% increase.                    → answered · calc.percent · from=-50 to=-25

Given: hint = none

[es] U: añade el iva a 100
     T: Dime el porcentaje, por ejemplo «100 más el 21% de IVA»: no supongo ninguna tasa.
                                                  → unhandled · calc.percent · amount=100
     (no rate is assumed (v0.1: a hint instead of a question))

Given: hint = en

[en] U: what's 50% of
     T: Give me the percentage and the amount, for example "15% of 80".
                                                  → unhandled · calc.percent · percent=50
     (missing amount (v0.1: a hint instead of a question))

Given: hint = none

[es] U: interés compuesto de 1000 al 3% durante 5 años
     T: No te he entendido.                       → unhandled · catch-all
     (not supported)
